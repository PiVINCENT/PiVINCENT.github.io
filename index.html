Welcome to my website!
I'm a PhD student in economics in Université Jean Monnet affiliated with GATE Lyon Saint-Étienne UMR 5824. I'm an applied microeconomist particularly interested in urban economics, spatial economics and climate change. My thesis lies at the intersection of urban and environmental economics. It is called The effect of urban overheating on urban configurations and is realized under the supervision of Florence GOFFETTE-NAGOT (ENS-Lyon) and Mathieu SANCH-MARITAN (UJM). My defense is planed for June 2027.
